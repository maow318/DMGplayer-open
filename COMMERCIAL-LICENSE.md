## Request a commercial license / 申请商业授权

Open a
[commercial license request](https://github.com/maow318/DMGplayer-open/issues/new?template=commercial-license.yml)
before publishing or submitting the software commercially. Do not include
confidential information in the public issue.

请在商业发布或提交审核前创建
[商业授权申请](https://github.com/maow318/DMGplayer-open/issues/new?template=commercial-license.yml)。
公开 Issue 中请勿填写机密或敏感信息。

the licensor will provide the applicable licensing fee and written commercial
terms. The fee is determined case by case. Opening an issue, discussing a
request, or receiving a quotation does not itself grant permission. Permission
exists only after the licensor has issued written authorization and all stated
conditions, including any required payment, have been satisfied.
